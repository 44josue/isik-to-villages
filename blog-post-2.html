Book Drive Week Recap: 500+ Books Collected

WH
Warris Hasan
Club Coordinator
May 10, 2026
4 min read

When we announced our first-ever book drive in late April, we set an internal goal of 200 donated items. We didn't tell anyone that number — we wanted to avoid the embarrassment of falling short publicly. One week later, we had to rent a second storage room.

Here's how the week went, what surprised us, and what we're changing for the next drive.

Setting Up the Drive

We placed four drop-off boxes across the Şile Campus: the main building foyer, the library entrance, the engineering block corridor, and the cafeteria near the exit. Each box had a sign explaining the initiative and listing what we needed — books, stationery, notebooks, pencils, rulers.

We promoted it through our Instagram page, a few posters, and word of mouth. We didn't have a budget for printing, so the posters were designed by Mert Kaan and printed at the campus print shop at cost. Total marketing spend: ₺40.

What People Actually Donated

The breakdown surprised us. We expected mostly notebooks and pencils. What we got was much richer:

  • 280 books — a mix of storybooks, science encyclopedias, and Turkish language readers. About 85% were in excellent condition.
  • 140 stationery packs — pencil cases, rulers, erasers, pencil sets.
  • 60 notebooks — mostly A4 lined, which is exactly what the schools asked for.
  • 32 items we had to decline — torn books, heavily annotated textbooks, or items too worn to pass on with dignity.

"I didn't think my old books would still be useful. I almost threw them out. Seeing the box made me walk back to my room and grab everything I hadn't opened in two years."

— Anonymous donor, Engineering Faculty Year 3

What We'll Do Differently Next Time

The drive worked. But there are things we'd change:

  • Clearer condition guidelines at the box. Several donors left damaged books in good faith. We need a small checklist on the box itself so people self-sort before donating.
  • A collection in the dormitories. Several students messaged us saying they had items but couldn't get to a drop-off point. A dorm pick-up day would help.
  • Earlier communication with teachers. Mehmet Bey in Ordu gave us a specific wish list after our visit. Next drive, we want to share that list with donors so contributions are targeted.

The next drive runs in October. If you have books collecting dust, hold onto them. We'll be back.
