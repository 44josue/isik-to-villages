1 Overview

Işık to Villages ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

This policy applies to information collected through our website, registration forms, donation forms, and suggestions form. By using our website or submitting any form, you consent to the practices described here.

We collect only what is necessary. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Ever.

2 Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide to us through the forms on our website:

Form Data Collected Required?
Club Registration Full name, Student ID, University email, Department, Motivation statement All required
Donation Name, email, donation amount (payment handled by third-party processor) Name & email required
Suggestions Name, email (optional), category, message Name & message required

Automatically Collected Data

When you visit our website, standard server logs may record your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time of visit. This data is used solely for site performance monitoring and is not linked to your identity.

3 How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide only for the following purposes:

  • Processing club membership applications and contacting applicants with decisions.
  • Sending donation receipts and impact updates to donors who opt in.
  • Reviewing suggestions and incorporating feedback into our programs.
  • Communicating important updates about club events and activities to registered members.
  • Maintaining internal records for university reporting requirements.

We will never use your information for marketing unrelated to Işık to Villages, and we will not contact you more than necessary.

4 Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may share limited information only in the following circumstances:

  • University Administration: We may share aggregated (non-personal) membership statistics with FMV Işık University as part of our club registration requirements.
  • Payment Processors: If you make a monetary donation, your payment details are handled directly by a third-party payment processor. We only receive confirmation of successful payment.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required by law or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights or the safety of others.

5 Data Storage & Security

Form submissions are currently stored securely and accessed only by authorized club leadership members. We take reasonable technical precautions to protect your data, including:

  • Access controls limiting who can view submitted data.
  • Use of HTTPS for all data transmission on this website.
  • Regular review of who has access to member and donor records.

However, as a student-operated initiative, we cannot guarantee enterprise-level security. We recommend not sharing sensitive personal information beyond what forms require.

Retention

We retain your data for as long as your membership is active or as needed to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

6 Cookies

Our website currently does not use tracking cookies or analytics cookies. If we add analytics tools in the future, we will update this policy and provide an opt-out mechanism.

Standard browser session cookies may be used to maintain site functionality, but these do not collect personal information and are deleted when you close your browser.

7 Children's Privacy

Our website and club membership are directed at university students (typically 18+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 16.

Our programs do serve children in rural schools, but we do not collect personal data from those children — only aggregate statistics (number of students reached, schools served) are recorded.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a minor, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

8 Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: You may request correction of any inaccurate data we hold.
  • Deletion: You may request that we delete your data, subject to any legal obligations to retain it.
  • Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw consent for us to contact you at any time by emailing us.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 10 business days.

9 Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, requests, or concerns, please reach out to us:

  • Email: isiktovillages@isik.edu.tr
  • Location: FMV Işık University, Şile Campus, Istanbul, Turkey
  • Instagram: @group4helpschools

We are a student team and aim to respond within 5–10 business days. Thank you for trusting us with your information.
