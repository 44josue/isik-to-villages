What We Built — Işık to Villages Website
New Pages
Page	What it does
terms.html	Terms of Use — 9 sections covering membership rules, donation policy, code of conduct, intellectual property, and liability. Includes a sticky sidebar table of contents.

privacy.html	Privacy Policy — explains what data the forms collect, how it's used, and users' rights. Includes a data table showing exactly which form collects what.

blog.html	Blog listing page — featured post card, 6 article cards in a grid, category filter tabs (Field Reports / Updates / Team / Essays), and a newsletter signup bar.

Blog pages	7 individual blog post pages (blog-post-1 through blog-post-7), one for each card on the blog listing. Each page includes a pull quote, a stats bar, tags, share buttons, and a sticky sidebar with TOC + related posts + donate CTA. Authors: Shema Josue, Warris Hasan, Mert Kaan, Rahima Noor, Baran Yöngel, İskender Tunç, Muhammad Hammad.

drive.html	Knowledge Drive "Coming Soon" page — students will eventually upload textbooks and notes for village schools to download. Shows a locked browser mockup preview, a 6-feature grid, a step-by-step how-it-works section, and an email notification form.

Homepage Additions (index.html)


Suggestions section	A two-column form section — left side explains four categories (Books, Events, Partnerships, Feedback), right side is the form. Submitting shows a success state without a page reload.

FAQ section	8-question accordion covering the most common donor and member questions (legitimacy, money trail, drop-off logistics, who can join, how to track impact). Opens one at a time.

Blog + Drive in navbar	Both new pages linked in the desktop and mobile navigation.

Footer — Legal column	Added a 4th footer column with links to Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, and Suggestions.

Footer — @project__404	Instagram handle added to all page footers, linked to https://www.instagram.com/project__404.
