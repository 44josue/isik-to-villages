What's Coming

Everything the Platform Will Do

The Knowledge Drive will be a dedicated space to share and access academic resources — built by students, for students and village schools alike.

Student Uploads

University students can upload their textbooks, lecture notes, past exam papers, and study guides directly to the platform in PDF or document format.

Planned

School Access Portal

Teachers and school administrators from partner village schools can browse, preview, and download any resource. No account required — completely free to access.

Planned

Subject & Grade Filtering

Resources are organized by subject area, grade level, and type (textbook, notes, exercises) so schools can find exactly what their students need.

Planned

Moderation & Quality Review

Every uploaded file goes through a review by our team before going live. We check for accuracy, completeness, and appropriateness for the intended grade level.

Planned

Impact Tracking

See how many times your uploaded materials have been downloaded, and which schools have accessed them. Know exactly how far your notes have traveled.

Planned

Contributor Recognition

Students whose resources are downloaded 50+ times earn a "Knowledge Contributor" badge and will be featured in our blog and end-of-year impact report.

Planned
The Process

How It Will Work

Simple for students to contribute. Simple for schools to use.

1

A Student Uploads a File

Any enrolled student logs in with their university email and uploads a PDF — a textbook, their semester notes, or a past exam. They add the subject, grade level, and a short description.

2

Our Team Reviews It

The file goes into a moderation queue. A club member reviews it within 48 hours to confirm it's appropriate, accurate, and complete. Approved files go live immediately.

3

Schools Browse and Download

Partner schools across Turkey can visit the Drive at any time, filter by subject or grade, and download anything they need — no registration, no cost, no limits.

4

Impact Gets Measured

Download counts are tracked and shared back with contributors, so students can see the direct reach of their materials. We publish an annual impact summary on the blog.
