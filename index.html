FAQ

Questions Answered

Everything donors, supporters, and potential members usually ask — answered in one place.

Still have questions? Email us
@group4helpschools

Yes. We are a registered student club operating under FMV Işık University (Şile Campus) as of 2026. All activities are supervised by the university and conducted in accordance with student club regulations. We are not a separate NGO — we are a university-affiliated initiative, which means our operations are transparent and accountable to the institution.

100% of monetary donations go directly toward purchasing books, stationery, and educational materials for village schools in our target regions (Ordu and Aydın provinces). Transportation costs for deliveries are covered separately through university support. We publish an impact update after every distribution — donors who share their email receive it directly.

Drop off your items at our collection point on FMV Işık University — Şile Campus. Look for the Işık to Villages drop-off box in the main building foyer. We accept books in good condition (no torn pages or missing covers), pencils, notebooks, rulers, and art supplies. Collection runs every weekday during term time.

Any currently enrolled student at FMV Işık University is welcome to apply. You don't need a specific major — we have members from engineering, business, education, and arts. Fill in the registration form in the Get Involved section and we'll get back to you within a week. Membership is free.

Not at all. Anyone can make a monetary donation or drop off supplies — you don't need to be affiliated with the university. If you're outside Istanbul and want to help, a monetary donation through our Donate section is the easiest way to contribute. Every amount helps.

We document every village visit with photos and a written field report, published on our blog. Contributors at the ₺400 tier and above receive a direct photo update from the school their materials reached. We also post regular updates on Instagram at @group4helpschools.

Our current focus is on under-resourced primary schools in Ordu and Aydın provinces in Turkey. We choose schools based on need assessments done in coordination with local teachers and administrators. As the club grows, we plan to expand to additional provinces.

Follow us on Instagram at @group4helpschools for real-time updates. For longer stories and field reports, check our blog. You can also subscribe to email updates in the blog's newsletter section — we send one email per major update, nothing more.
