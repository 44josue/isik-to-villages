1 Introduction

Welcome to Işık to Villages ("we," "us," or "our"), a student-led social initiative operating under FMV Işık University. By accessing our website, submitting a registration form, making a donation, or participating in any of our programs, you agree to be bound by these Terms of Use.

These terms govern your relationship with Işık to Villages and outline your rights and responsibilities as a visitor, member, or supporter of our initiative. Please read them carefully.

Işık to Villages is a non-commercial student club. All activities are conducted in good faith for the purpose of supporting rural education in Turkey. We are not a licensed NGO or registered charity.

2 Club Membership

By submitting the registration form on our website, you are expressing your interest in joining the Işık to Villages club. Membership is subject to approval by the club's organizing team.

Eligibility

  • Membership is open to currently enrolled students of FMV Işık University.
  • All applicants must provide a valid university email address ending in @isik.edu.tr.
  • Applicants must be in good academic standing as defined by the university.

Member Responsibilities

  • Members are expected to actively participate in club activities and events.
  • Members must represent the club in a professional and respectful manner.
  • Members must respect the privacy and dignity of the communities we serve.
  • Membership may be revoked for conduct that is deemed harmful to the club or its mission.

3 Donations

Donations and contributions made to Işık to Villages — whether monetary or in-kind — are used exclusively to support the educational programs and supply drives described on our website.

Monetary Donations

  • All monetary donations are processed securely. We do not store payment card information on our servers.
  • Donations are non-refundable unless there was a technical error in processing.
  • Tier selections (Supporter, Contributor, Champion) are indicative of expected impact and do not constitute a legally binding contract.

In-Kind Donations (Books, Stationery)

  • Physical donations dropped off at our campus collection points become the property of Işık to Villages for distribution.
  • We reserve the right to assess the condition of donated items and decline items that are damaged or unsuitable.
  • We cannot guarantee that specific donated items will be delivered to a specific recipient school.

4 Code of Conduct

All participants, members, and users of this website are expected to uphold the following standards of conduct:

  • Treat all individuals — team members, school communities, donors, and visitors — with respect and dignity.
  • Do not submit false, misleading, or fraudulent information in any form on this website.
  • Do not attempt to gain unauthorized access to any part of this website or our systems.
  • Do not use our platforms for commercial solicitation, spam, or unauthorized advertising.
  • Respect the privacy of children and communities featured in our content — do not redistribute personal information about beneficiaries.

Violations of this code may result in removal from club activities, revocation of membership, and reporting to university authorities if warranted.

5 Website Content

The information on this website — including project descriptions, donation goals, statistics, and program details — is provided in good faith for informational purposes. We strive for accuracy but cannot guarantee that all information is current, complete, or error-free.

Funding goals (e.g., ₺50,000 campaign target) and progress figures are updated periodically and may not reflect real-time data. Statistics about students reached and books collected are estimates based on ongoing programs.
